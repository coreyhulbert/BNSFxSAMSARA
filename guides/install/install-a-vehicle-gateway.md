<p>Samsara Vehicle Gateways (VGs) provide cloud connectivity for real-time and historical tracking of sensor data. All Vehicle Gateway models come with the device, corresponding cable for your diagnostic port, and mounting accessories.</p>
    <p>After you activate your VG in the Samsara dashboard, you can install it on your asset. Watch the VG Installation Video for your model, or follow the steps below.</p>
    <h3>To install a VG</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>Unbox the VG.</li>
      <li>Confirm you have the correct hardware and installation guide for your scenario (accessories for your configuration; the install guide for your VG model and cable type).</li>
      <li>Use the Samsara Fleet App to streamline gateway onboarding.</li>
      <li>Select an appropriate install location, following the requirements in the install guide for your model and installation type.</li>
      <li>Connect one end of the provided cable to the vehicle's diagnostic port (typically below the steering column), and the other end to the VG.</li>
      <li>Observe the light on the gateway connection port. Initialization can take up to three minutes — orange during initialization, green once connected to the Samsara Cloud.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="callout tip"><strong>Tip</strong><p>Secure the VG tightly to the vehicle. A loose VG may cause inconsistent Harsh Event detection. Avoid surrounding the VG with metal parts, which can interfere with wireless or GPS connectivity.</p></div>
    <div class="callout tip"><strong>Tip</strong><p>Avoid wrapping the power and device cables around the gateway, and avoid tight bends in the cables.</p></div>
    <div class="callout note"><strong>Note</strong><p>If the light flashes orange and never turns green after three minutes: verify the gateway has a clear view of the sky and isn't obstructed (e.g., not in a garage); perform a hard reboot by unplugging for 15 minutes, then plugging back in and taking the vehicle on a short trip; contact Support if the location still doesn't report.</p></div>
    <h3>Next steps</h3>
    <p>Once installed, configure the gateway in the Samsara dashboard: name the gateway, manage VG device settings, pair the gateway with an asset. To make a later change: replace a VG with the same model, move a VG between vehicles, delete a vehicle, or deactivate a VG.</p>
