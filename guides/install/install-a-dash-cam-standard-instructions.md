<p>Use these steps for standard installation of dash cam models manufactured prior to the CM33 and CM34. For CM33/CM34 models, refer to the CM33/CM34 Dash Cam Installation Guide instead.</p>
    <h3>What you'll need</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>1x Dash Cam</li>
      <li>1x Vehicle Gateway</li>
      <li>1x Cleaning wipe</li>
      <li>1x Allen key</li>
      <li>4x Cable clips</li>
    </ul>
    <h3>Installation steps</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>Identify the mounting location and clean it thoroughly with the provided wipe. Position the camera below the rear-view mirror and above the windshield sweep for optimal visibility and AI Event Detection accuracy.</li>
      <li>Peel off the protective sheet and firmly press the dash cam against the windshield, ensuring no large bubbles between adhesive and glass.</li>
      <li>Adjust the camera angle so it hangs perfectly vertical, then tighten both screws with the hex key.</li>
      <li>Run the cable across the top of the windshield and secure it with cable clips, or tuck it under the glass seal.</li>
      <li>Plug the camera into the USB port of the Vehicle Gateway. For best results, the cable shouldn't wrap around the gateway or cross the diagnostics cable.</li>
      <li>Verify the installation: confirm the new dash cam displays under Settings → Devices → Cameras, then use Test Camera Positioning to confirm placement.</li>
    </ol>
    <h3>Next steps</h3>
    <p>Configure dash cam settings and alerts (which images/video are captured, and a Dash Cam Disconnected alert), and review recorded images and video from completed trips on the Trip Timeline. If images look blurry or obstructed, refer to the Camera Lens Cleaning Guidelines.</p>
